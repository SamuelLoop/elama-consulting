How It Works

One sector, one advisory partner, one underwriter

Each governance module can carry a single sponsored position per sector — an advisory firm's sign-off, paired with an underwriter's cover, both attached to every automated check MPowa runs under that module.

  1. 1

    A sector opens

    MPowa scopes a governance module to a named regulatory sector — for example, FCA Rule Breaker to UK financial services, or GDPR Compliance to enterprise data handling.

  2. 2

    A partner takes the position

    One advisory firm and one underwriting partner take the paid, exclusive position for that sector, reviewing and co-signing the module's rule interpretation before it goes live.

  3. 3

    Every check carries both signatures

    Every agent check run under that module is scoped to the advisory partner's sign-off and backed by the underwriter's cover — both logged to the Anchor Ledger alongside MPowa's own verdict.

Combined Cover

Regulatory sign-off and underwritten cover, sold together

The sponsored position price bundles the advisory partner's rule sign-off with a base tranche of underwritten liability cover for agentic AI activity in that sector, priced and backed by the position's underwriting partner.

What a sector position includes
  • Exclusive advisory sign-off on that module's rule interpretation for the sector
  • A base tranche of underwritten liability cover, priced by the position's underwriter
  • Both signatures logged to the Anchor Ledger on every check MPowa runs under that module
  • First right of renewal on the sector position, reviewed annually
