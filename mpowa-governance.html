Why MPowa Exists

Agentic AI has no flight recorder

Autonomous agents are already making consequential decisions inside regulated businesses — and most leave no tamper-proof, independently verifiable record of what they did or why.

No record survives the agent

When something goes wrong, there's rarely a trustworthy account of what the agent actually did, in what order, or who — if anyone — was watching.

Regulation has a deadline. Infrastructure doesn't.

New AI regulation increasingly requires documented oversight of automated decisions — but mandating oversight doesn't create the plumbing to deliver it.

Training alone doesn't fix it

Safety training reduces bad outcomes; it doesn't produce the verifiable record of what an agent actually did that an audit or a regulator will ask for.

How It Works

Every action, captured, ordered, and anchored

The agent checks aren't a black box. Every step is written to the Anchor Ledger, a three-layer record designed to survive scrutiny.

  1. 1

    Capture

    Every action an agent takes — a tool call, a message to another agent, a human stepping in, a safety trigger — is written to an append-only log within milliseconds.

  2. 2

    Order

    Actions are sealed into linked batches, each one cryptographically bound to the one before it, so nothing can be quietly edited, removed, or reordered after the fact.

  3. 3

    Anchor

    A fingerprint of each batch is committed to a public ledger — independent proof that doesn't rely on taking MPowa's word for it.

The Difference

Simple, automated, provable
